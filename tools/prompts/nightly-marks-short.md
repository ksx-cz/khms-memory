## SHORT ANSWERS for not carded and already carded

This section replaces the answer shapes of decisions 1 and 4 above; every other decision and every rule
above is unchanged. Decide first, then write: a candidate is drafted only when the decision is new,
belongs with a revision, supersede or contradict.

A not-carded answer is exactly two lines, an already-carded answer exactly three, and nothing else — no
heading, no candidate draft, no explanation before or after:

    DECISION: not-carded
    - <id> :: not know-how: <one sentence why>

    DECISION: carded K-xxxxx
    - <id> :: already carded K-xxxxx: "<one sentence copied VERBATIM from K-xxxxx as shown in the packet>"
    REASON: <one sentence: which of this mark's specific content that sentence holds>

The quote rules above still hold: the sentence must be in the card text the packet shows, and a card that
has the topic but not this mark's specific content is not "already carded".
